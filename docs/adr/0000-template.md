# ADR-0000: Title of the decision

Samenvatting (NL): Een regel Nederlands die de beslissing samenvat.

## Status

One of `Accepted` (the default of the decision brief stands), `Adjusted` (a probe or a check disproved
part of it and the replacement is stated), or `Plan` (decided in the plan, not in the decision brief).
Write the word first, then, if useful, "with changes" or the reason. A superseded ADR keeps its number
and says by which ADR it was replaced.

Source: where the decision was made (a row of plan section 6, a task, a spec requirement).

## Context

What forced a decision: the requirement, the constraint, the failure that was seen. Name the evidence
IDs (V-nn, P-nn, A-nn, BC-nn, OD-nn, R-nn).

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and with exact versions and names where they matter.

## Consequences

What follows: costs, limits, what must be revisited and when. Say what stays unverified.

## Evidence

- What was run or read, where (a gate note, a test file, a command), and what it showed.
- What was not verified, said plainly.
