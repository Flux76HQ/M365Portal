## Assumptions
A-1 One logo serves both themes; light logo for the light theme, else the other; compact for the collapsed sidebar and the header below 768 px. A-2 The logo or tile is decorative (empty alt, aria-hidden) while the name is written beside it. A-3 `/` and Ctrl or Cmd+K both open the palette (PD-18). A-4 Actor name and initials come from the actor kind translated in the page language, not the server's English label. A-5 Preview banner is at the top of the content column and scrolls away; the header is sticky. A-6 `Access` gained a third verdict `missing` (route absent for this server, shows Not-found); `throw notFound()` is refused by lint (`only-throw-error`) and disables are forbidden. A-7 DataTable labels live in `common.table.*` (no new namespace). A-8 Key names whose last segment is `one`, `two` etc. are plural forms (the catalogue check caught `showcase.sample.one`).
